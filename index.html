Hello, I'm ADH

I'm a freelance graphic designer and illustrator based wherever creativity takes me. For over a decade I've been helping brands, publishers, and independent clients turn their ideas into distinctive visual experiences.

My work spans brand identity, editorial illustration, packaging, and hand-lettering. I believe the best design is honest, precise, and full of character — work that people feel before they read.

When I'm not at the drawing board, you'll find me sketching in coffee shops, collecting vintage posters, or hosting illustration workshops for emerging creatives.
